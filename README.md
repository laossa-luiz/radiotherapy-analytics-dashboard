🔗 **[(GitHub Pages)](https://github.com/laossa-luiz)**

---

### 📌 Business & Clinical Context
In radiation oncology, monitoring patient wait times, linear accelerator (LINAC) capacity, treatment techniques, and dose compliance is critical for clinical efficiency and quality of care. 

This project simulates a real-world Healthcare Intelligence application, tracking 160+ patient records, treatment plans (VMAT, IMRT, SBRT, 3D-CRT), and operational metrics.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

- **Interactive KPIs:** Real-time tracking of Active Patients, Total Radiation Sessions, Wait Times (Diagnosis to 1st Fraction), and LINAC Occupancy/DQA Pass Rates.
- **Automated Clinical Insights:** JavaScript engine generating real-time operational alerts and top-performing treatment stats.
- **Multi-Dimensional Dynamic Filters:** Filter data simultaneously by Period/Year, Medical Unit, Tumor Site (Breast, Prostate, Lung, Head & Neck, Pelvis, CNS), Technique, LINAC Equipment, and Healthcare Payor (SUS, Private).
- **Interactive Data Visualization:** Custom Chart.js charts including Monthly Sessions Trend, Tumor Site Distribution (Donut Chart), Technique Breakdown, LINAC Utilization, and Wait Time by Payor.
- **Data Table & Modal Details:** Full tabular record tracking with multi-column sorting, real-time search, pagination, individual patient modal popups, and CSV data export.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries

- **Frontend Structure:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS (Dark Mode Glassmorphism UI)
- **Data Engine & Logic:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Seeded Random Data Generator
- **Data Visualization:** Chart.js 4.x
